PXL SEQ / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

PXL Seq for iPad is a native control surface developed by Tomislav Rupic / Pixel Records. It controls the PXL Seq instrument on your Mac; sequencing and MIDI output run on that Mac. This policy covers the iPad controller and this PXL Seq project website, not other Pixel Records products.

Privacy contact: info@tomislav-rupic.com.

Connection and musical information

The app does not require a Pixel Records account. It has no advertising, session replay or embedded third-party analytics SDK. Your selected Mac receives the settings, note and controller values, phrase names, tree structures, playback and routing information needed to control the instrument. These messages are not sent to a Pixel Records cloud service.

The Mac endpoint address and private pairing credential are stored in the iPad Keychain, with device-only accessibility while unlocked. Keep pairing codes and links private. Network providers you choose may process connection information under their own policies.

The current wireless setup uses Tailscale as a separate application and service, not an embedded SDK. See Tailscale's privacy policy. PXL Seq does not create a Tailscale account for you.

Camera

Camera access is optional, for scanning your Mac's pairing QR code. The code is processed on the iPad; camera images are not saved or uploaded by PXL Seq. You may paste the pairing link instead and change camera permission in iPad Settings.

Files and your compositions

When you import a tree file, its contents are sent to your selected Mac. Tree and MIDI exports are saved to the location you select. Providers such as iCloud Drive may synchronize these files according to your settings and their policies. PXL Seq does not automatically upload your musical library to Pixel Records.

Retention and removal

Use Pair Mac > Forget Mac to remove the saved pairing, including while the Mac is offline. The app reports removal errors. Deleting the app alone may not remove a Keychain item. You can revoke the old controller credential on the Mac companion's pairing page.

Unpairing does not delete compositions or history on your Mac, or files you exported. Those remain under your control and must be removed in their respective locations.

TestFlight and support

For TestFlight installations, Apple collects beta diagnostics and usage information and shares information with the developer. Feedback you submit may include screenshots and contact information. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

If you email support, we receive your address and the information you choose to send. Never include private pairing credentials or compositions you do not intend to share. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request, not to add you to marketing lists.

Routine support correspondence is deleted within 90 days after resolution, except records required for legal obligations.

This website

This project website uses system fonts and locally hosted styles, scripts and images. It does not embed analytics, session replay or a registration system. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub privacy statement.

Following an external link, emailing support or using an external network/file provider involves that provider's own services and policies. The website does not silently connect your browser to your studio instrument.

Requests and changes

Contact info@tomislav-rupic.com with privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete information you have sent us. Rights depend on applicable law, and some records may need to be retained for legal obligations. You may also contact your relevant data protection authority.

Updates will be dated here. Material changes to hosting, analytics, account features or data handling require a new review of these disclosures.
